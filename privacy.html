Privacy Policy — SplatScan

Last updated: November 24, 2026

SplatScan is an app that scans an object or a room with your phone’s camera and turns it into a 3D model (a “Gaussian splat”) on your phone. It is developed by Nathan Dickson (“we”, “us”).

Summary

SplatScan does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your photos, scans, and 3D models stay on your phone. We have no servers and no access to your content.

Camera and ARCore

The app asks for camera permission so you can scan. While you scan, the app uses Google’s ARCore (provided by Google Play Services for AR) to track the phone’s position. ARCore processes camera images on your phone for this purpose, and it is provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The frames the app saves for a scan, and the 3D model made from them, are kept in the app’s private storage on your phone.

Data storage

Scans, training data, 3D models, and scenes are stored locally in the app’s private storage area. The app opts out of Android’s automatic app backup, so it does not copy them to cloud backups. You can delete any scan or 3D model inside the app (Settings has options to delete everything), and uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Network use

SplatScan does not connect to the internet. It has no network permission and makes no network requests of its own. Links in the app (for example to the Google Privacy Policy) open in your device’s browser — that is your browser making the request, not the app.

Sharing and files

If you export or share a 3D model or a scan, the app hands the file to the app you choose through Android’s share sheet; we do not see where it goes. Importing a file uses your device’s built-in file picker, which only gives the app the specific file you pick. The app never requests access to your photo library or storage.

Location (optional, off by default)

In Settings you can switch on “Record where scans are taken”. If you do, and you grant location permission, the app notes the phone’s approximate position (latitude, longitude and accuracy) once when you start recording a scan, and saves it in that scan’s folder on your phone. It is used only to show where the scan was made (“Show where it was taken” opens your maps app). It is never sent anywhere, and it is left out of the data you export or share from the app. Switching the setting off stops new locations being recorded; you can delete a scan to delete its location.

Notifications

While a 3D model is being trained, the app shows an ongoing notification so training can keep running when the app is not on screen. The notification contains no personal data, and you can decline the notification permission.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.
